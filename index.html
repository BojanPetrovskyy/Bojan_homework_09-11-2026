Icecrown Citadel:
The Icecrown Citadel is the final raid instance in Wrath of the Lich King, and its final boss is none other than Arthas Menethil, the Lich King himself! Similarly to the Black Temple and Mount Hyjal, players will be assisted by a special raid-only faction: the Ashen Verdict. Teleportation devices placed in various points inside the Citadel will allow raiders to quickly move around the enormous building, and Alchemist Finklestein, Ashen Verdict Quartermaster, will aid in ensuring that player armor is always at its maximum durability.

I read that on all browsers it should work, but modern way to achieve such things is in CSS instead. This was sole AI use for this exercise.
  Even though it was specified to use nested lists in the exercise I wanted to go deeper just for fun and went ahead and added absolute hyperlink urls to each item with _blank target attibute, description lists and anchors for ease of navigation.
  For the sake of looks i tried to use 'br' tag directly into the nested lists but after it was finished i ran an AI check, so I came to a conclusion that it is a wrong usage and spacing is CSS job later, so 'br' tags we're removed from inside the list elements.
